Tech Guide

  • Tension: Recommended 22-26kg for control, 20-23kg for power.
  • Pattern: 16x19 offers more spin, 18x20 offers more control.
  • String Type: Poly = durability/spin, Multifilament = comfort/power.
  • Hybrid: Poly main for spin control, multifilament cross for comfort.
  • Maintenance: Restring every 20-40 playing hours or monthly for regular players.
